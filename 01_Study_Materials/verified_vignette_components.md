# Verified design components, not a verbatim transcript

This file records components preserved in the timestamped design discussion. It is not a substitute for the deployed English vignette text.

## Standard factor slots

- Body: original human body versus cat body.
- Memory: all pre-procedure memories retained versus no pre-procedure memories retained.
- Function: ordinary conversation/daily action retained versus limited communication/function.
- Self-identification: affirms being X, is uncertain whether it is X, or denies being X.
- Transition: uninterrupted transformation versus complete stopping of the prior activity followed by restart from a stored record.
- Standard conditions stated that two or more individuals did not coexist during the procedure.

## Parallel anchor

The verified conceptual structure was: X's state is recorded; X receives no further processing and continues living in the original human body; a separate process uses the record to generate a cat-bodied individual with X's recorded memories, retained abilities, and affirmative self-identification.

## Deny anchor

All non-self-identification cues were held at the baseline level while the post-procedure individual stated that it was not X.
