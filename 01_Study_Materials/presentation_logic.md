# Presentation and arm logic

- Each arm-session presented all 10 conditions within participant.
- The eight standard conditions (`C01`–`C08`) were randomized.
- The two extreme anchors (`A09`, `A10`) were inserted only in positions 5–10. The recorded session orders verify that neither anchor appeared first.
- The condition text was shared across arms; the item set differed by arm.
- Manipulation arm: five perceived-continuity items after every condition, with copy/termination items for discontinuous and Parallel conditions and a coexistence item for Parallel.
- Judgment arm: five query items for standard and Deny conditions. Parallel replaced the ambiguous identity/likeness items with three pairwise same-person judgments and an exclusivity item while retaining relationship, obligation, and treatment.
- `position` in the response data records the realized 1–10 presentation position.

The exact deployed randomization code must be copied into `Source_Code/` before public OSF release.
