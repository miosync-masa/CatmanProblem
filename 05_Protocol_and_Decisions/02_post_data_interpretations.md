# Post-data interpretations and future hypotheses

These interpretations are exploratory and must not be presented as preregistered predictions.

## Transition as activity interruption

The discontinuous wording successfully reduced perceived transition continuity but produced little change in the five judgment outcomes. The most conservative interpretation is not that transition is irrelevant. The intervention may have isolated interruption of ongoing activity, a discontinuity humans routinely assimilate to sleep, anesthesia, or temporary unconsciousness.

## Learned biological manifold

One post-study hypothesis is that ordinary judgment rules are trained on a biological manifold `M_bio`. Activity interruption lies inside or near that manifold, whereas a cat body is far outside it. Projection back toward familiar biological cases could therefore compress the effective transition manipulation while preserving a large body intervention.

## Transition may not be one dimension

The transition construct may divide into at least:

- activity continuity;
- carrier/token continuity;
- lineage or provenance continuity; and
- uniqueness/non-branching continuity.

The current study primarily changed the first and partly evoked the others. A future study should manipulate these components independently.

## Query-specific structure

Body, Memory, Self-ID uncertainty, and Deny produced statistically heterogeneous query profiles. This result motivated a sharper formulation of the Cat-Man Problem as a general diagnostic form of inquiry: ask whether an apparently unitary natural-language predicate is actually a bundle of query-indexed judgments.

## Deny

The observed Deny pattern was closer to a relationship/obligation/treatment shift than to a complete identity veto. This is compatible with a distinction between epistemic identity judgment and normative respect for self-identification, but the current study does not establish a causal mediation pathway.

## Parallel

Joint-high predecessor links and transitivity-like discordance were observed in a minority of participants. These are existence/proof-of-concept observations, not precise prevalence estimates and not evidence that mathematical identity itself is non-transitive.
