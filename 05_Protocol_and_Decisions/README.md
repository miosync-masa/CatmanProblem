# Protocol and decision record

This directory separates design commitments made before viewing the completed dataset from interpretations and operational decisions made after data collection. The study was not formally preregistered; “pre-data” means documented in the design discussion before the final response data were inspected, not preregistered confirmation.

- `01_pre_data_decisions.md`: design and construct decisions.
- `02_post_data_interpretations.md`: hypotheses generated or sharpened after seeing the results.
- `03_analysis_decisions.md`: exact operational definitions used in this public analysis package.
